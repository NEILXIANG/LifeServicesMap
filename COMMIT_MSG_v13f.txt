fix: first-time-user bug sweep — 25 defects across phone, tablet and desktop (v2026.09.13f)

Three audits driven as a first-time visitor (390/320/landscape phone, 820/768/
1024 tablet, 1280/1920 desktop + keyboard, six languages). Bugs only; no
behaviour was added. Every fix is checked by regress round 76 (27 checks).

Logic / crashes
- Typing an arrival date digit by digit fired change with a half-typed year
  ("0002-09-01"); the tax deadline became an Invalid Date and fmtDate's own
  fallback threw — uncaught, "page hit an error" toast — and a stored partial
  year made every later guide open throw. Only a real date between 1990 and
  today is accepted (validArrival); a future or partial value is ignored and
  the field shows what is stored once it loses focus. fmtDate guards NaN.
- fmtDist: 949 m → "950 m", 950 → "0.9 km", 951 → "1.0 km" (binary halves in
  toFixed). Rounds on an integer first; the scale is monotonic.
- Ticking a step did not refresh the landing progress strip (only marking a
  whole card did) — it appeared after the next reload.

Navigation / history
- Guide opened from a link (flyer QR, ?task=): "← All tasks" then another card
  then Back left the site. The list now takes the guide's history entry at that
  point, so Back returns to the list and the next Back closes the guide.
- Closing the guide with a card open, then reopening, showed the stale card
  with no entry under it (Back closed the whole guide). Reopens on the list.
- Double tap on the guide/share button: the second tap hit the backdrop that
  had just slid in and closed the sheet. Backdrop taps in the first 400 ms are
  ignored.
- Swipe-down on the sheet header never dismissed (pointercancel: the browser
  claimed the gesture) — touch-action:none on .guide-head, as on the handle.

Map card vs route bar / notes vs locate button
- Choosing a route from a place card laid the route bar over the card's name,
  ★ and ✕ (phone and tablet ≤900). panPad/popMaxH now include the bar; an open
  card is re-laid out and nudged clear (relayoutOpenPopup), also on rotate —
  which previously clipped the card's header above the map.
- Offline note ✕ was under the 48 px locate button on a Chinese phone (tapping
  it opened the location explainer); the explainer's "Not now" was half under
  it. The corner's height varies (attribution wraps), so placeMapNotes()
  measures the control and lifts both notes above it (--note-lift).
- 601–900 px: the zoom control was drawn over the start of the route bar.
- A wide landmark label could sit fully over a small cluster bubble and hide
  its count; slots that leave every bubble readable are tried first.
- "Open it on the map" on 1024×768 left the card's action row below the fold
  (Leaflet pans inside the map, not the window): the page scrolls to show it.

Layout / targets
- 390 px phone: the place count was squeezed into 16 px ("2…", "全"); it now
  wraps to the second row as its comment always said.
- Landing strip: nowrap on the deadline made the count one glyph per line
  (8 lines in Chinese) and pushed the date off screen. The row wraps.
- Filter sheets share one scrolling body: a scrolled Category opened Bus
  Routes 400 px down with its search box off screen. A new section starts at
  its top (after the panel is displayed — a hidden one ignores scrollTop).
- 1024×600 landscape tablet: the panel's Done bar sat 45 px below the screen;
  positionPanel caps the height to what is left below the button.
- Flyer landing card ≥700 px: heading was empty (.fu-hs is hidden there) and
  the 44 px ✕ target reached into the first button. Heading rendered plain and
  as tall as the ✕.
- Korean/Japanese title broke inside a word at 601–660 px (word-break:keep-all).
- Popup ✕ was Leaflet's 24 px on phones — the only exit when the card fills
  the map; 40 px on coarse pointers. Favourites row: the 16 px name button now
  takes the row's full 44 px; ✕ 32 px.
- Debug panel (?debug=1) was drawn over the guide sheet (z 99999 → 1150) and
  its event count never refreshed.
- Favourites import/export message kept the old language after a switch.

Not changed (deliberate, needs a decision): place markers stay out of the tab
order (regress check 42), so a keyboard-only user cannot open a place card
from the map.
