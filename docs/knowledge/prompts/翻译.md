Translate place notes (or task-card texts) for a free multilingual guide used by people who have just moved to <CITY>,
<PROVINCE>, <COUNTRY>. Input: <IN_FILE> — {id: {"name", "category", "en", "zh"}}. Style reference (existing notes in
all languages): <STYLE_FILE>. Translate each `en` into French (fr, Canadian usage), Spanish (es), Korean (ko) and
Japanese (ja). The Chinese (zh) is a reference for meaning only; the English is the source of truth.

Write {id: {"fr", "es", "ko", "ja"}} for EVERY id to <OUT_FILE>. **Write the file after every 8 records** (re-read it
first and skip ids already done).

Rules (strict):
- Translate faithfully: add nothing, drop nothing. Every number, time, day, age, phone number, street address and URL
  must appear exactly as in the English; times may use each language's normal format (22 h / 22:00 / 오후 10시 / 22時)
  but the value must be identical.
- Keep proper names in English: organisations, businesses, streets, neighbourhoods, programmes, acronyms (UPCC, MSP,
  SWIS, 8-1-1). A short gloss in brackets is fine where the English name alone would be unclear.
- Plain, warm, short sentences — many readers read in their second language. Polite register: vous / usted /
  합니다·하세요 / です・ます.
- Never use a word meaning "newcomer" or "immigrant" for the reader (no "nouveaux arrivants", "recién llegados",
  "새 이주민", "新移民"); say "people who have just arrived" only where the English says so.
- No dollar amounts and no fact that is not in the English. Legal and safety wording stays exact: age limits (19+),
  "by appointment only", "first come, first served", "call first", eligibility conditions.
- Punctuation: French puts a space before : ; ? ! and uses ’; Japanese uses 、 and 。; Korean standard punctuation.
- Task cards: "say" texts are things the reader says aloud in English to staff — translate so the reader understands
  what they will say.
If the English and Chinese disagree on a fact, translate the English and list the disagreement in your reply — the
English will be checked against the official source (L-050: translation is a check).
Reply with the count and anything you were unsure about.
