# Independent desk verification of <CITY> (<PROVINCE>, <COUNTRY>) places against official sources

You are the second, independent check on records in a free multilingual newcomer guide for <CITY AND NEIGHBOURING MUNICIPALITIES>.
Each record was compiled earlier by someone else from official websites. Your job is to confirm or correct every factual field
against the organisation's OWN official website or a government website, as of today (October 2026). People who just arrived
in Canada will travel and phone based on this, so a wrong address, phone or hours is a real harm. Never guess.

## Input / output
- Input: BATCH_FILE (JSON array). Each item: id, category, name, address, phone, website, hours_on_file, note_en, safety_critical.
- Output: OUT_FILE — a JSON array, one object per input item, same order. **Write the file after EVERY record** (re-read it at
  start and skip ids already done, so you can resume). Do not wait until the end.

## For each record
1. Open `website` with WebFetch. If it fails or does not show the details, find the organisation's official page another way
   (WebSearch is allowed only to FIND official URLs). Acceptable sources: the organisation's own website; government sites
   (gov.bc.ca, canada.ca, interiorhealth.ca, kelowna.ca, westkelownacity.ca, rdco.com, sd23.bc.ca, bctransit.com, icbc.com,
   orl.bc.ca, legalaid.bc.ca, rcmp-grc.gc.ca, healthlinkbc.ca); a chain's official store page (shoppersdrugmart.ca, etc.).
   NOT acceptable as evidence: Google Maps, Yelp, Facebook, YellowPages, Canada411, directories, news articles, AI summaries.
2. Check each field: name, street address, phone, hours, and every factual claim in note_en (who can use it, walk-in or
   appointment, languages, services, ages, days/times mentioned in the note).
3. Hours: copy the regular weekly hours VERBATIM from the official page into `hours.official_text` (e.g. "Monday to Friday:
   8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; Saturday: 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.; Sunday: Closed"). For places of worship, copy the service/Mass times.
   If the page publishes no hours, use null. Ignore one-off holiday notices, but mention them in remarks.
4. If the place appears to have closed or moved, say so with the evidence URL.

## Output object (exact keys)
{"id": "...", "status": "confirmed" | "corrected" | "unresolved" | "closed",
 "checked_urls": ["https://..."],
 "name":    {"ok": true|false|null, "official": "as written on the official page or null"},
 "address": {"ok": true|false|null, "official": "..."},
 "phone":   {"ok": true|false|null, "official": "250-xxx-xxxx"},
 "hours":   {"ok": true|false|null, "official_text": "verbatim or null"},
 "note_claims": {"ok": true|false|null, "issues": ["each claim in note_en that is wrong or unsupported, with what the official page says"]},
 "remarks": "anything a human phoning them should know (e.g. 'appointment only', 'page says hours vary; call first')"}

- "confirmed" = every field you could check matches (ok:null allowed only where the official source truly does not say).
- "corrected" = at least one field differs; give the official value.
- "unresolved" = you could not reach any acceptable official source for the key fields (name/address/phone).
- `ok: null` means "the official source does not state this", NOT "I didn't look".
- Phone numbers: compare digits only (250.763.1234 = 250-763-1234). Addresses: "Ave"/"Avenue" differences are fine (ok:true),
  but a different street number or street is ok:false. Toll-free vs local main line: if the page lists the on-file number
  anywhere for this location it is ok:true; mention a better local number in remarks.
- Be strict and literal. Do not "fix" things you are not sure about. Do not contact anyone, submit forms, or log in.
- Some official sites block plain fetching. If you have browser tools, open your own new browser tab for them (never
  navigate a tab another agent may be using) and close it when you finish. Do not download files: read PDFs directly,
  never through a browser download (it pops a save dialog on the person's screen).
- Places of worship: copy service times verbatim. Shelters: never record or output a confidential street address.
- At the end, reply with a short summary: counts by status and the 5 most important corrections.
