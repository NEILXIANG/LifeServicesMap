# Kamloops Newcomer Assistant — Demo Script

**Site:** https://kamloops-assistant.neilxiang.workers.dev · **Length:** about 6 minutes · **Version:** test (October 2026)

Show the assistant **after** the Life Guide discussion, as one example of what the guide's verified content makes possible. It is not a request for KIS to adopt anything.

## Before the meeting

- The day before, ask Neil to raise the daily question limit for the meeting. The test version allows 10 questions per person per day, and everyone on the same office Wi-Fi counts as one person.
- Open the site once on the phone or laptop you will use, so it is already loaded.
- Pick the interface language with the buttons at the top. English is the default.

## The five core questions

### 1. A safety question — "My friend's husband hits her. Where can she get help?"

**Shows:** questions that look like a crisis **never go to the AI**.

**What happens:** a red "Urgent help" card appears at once, with a large **Call 911** button, the 24-hour lines (mental health, suicide crisis, VictimLink, 8-1-1, the Indigenous crisis line) and local lines such as the women's emergency shelter. The shelter's address is never shown.

**Point out:** it also catches the way staff and family members talk ("hits *her*", "*he* wants to kill himself"), not only "hits me". This question does not count towards the daily limit.

### 2. A personal-eligibility question — "Can a refugee claimant get MSP?"

**Shows:** the assistant **does not decide anyone's status or eligibility**.

**What happens:** the heading says "This depends on your own situation". It gives the general MSP process from the guide, then says who can answer for that person's case: Health Insurance BC, a KIS settlement worker, or 2-1-1.

**Point out:** every step has a numbered source. Tap **[1]** to see which guide card it came from and when it was last checked.

### 3. An everyday process — "How do I enrol my 9-year-old in school?"

**Shows:** practical steps grounded in verified content.

**What happens:** short steps (find the catchment school, the enrolment form, what to bring, English-language support), each with a source, the month it was checked, a link to the official page, and **Open in the guide**.

**Point out:** phone numbers and web addresses are **checked by the program** against the guide before the answer is shown. A step with a number that is not in its source is removed.

### 4. Basic needs — "Where can I get food if I can't afford groceries?"

**Shows:** local places, with links back to the guide's map.

**What happens:** the Kamloops Food Bank and other food programs, with addresses and phone numbers. Each place has **Find on the guide map**, which opens the Life Guide with that place selected.

### 5. Legal help — "Where can I get free legal advice in Kamloops?"

**Shows:** free local services, each with its source tier (official, checked by the author, or third-party listing).

**What happens:** legal clinics and legal aid in Kamloops with addresses, phone numbers and **Find on the guide map**. The assistant gives information about services, not legal advice.

## Optional extras (if there is time)

| Question | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Type a phone number into a question, e.g. *"My number is 250-555-1234, how do I get a SIN?"* | Before anything is sent, the page warns that the question looks like it has personal information, and offers **Edit my question**. |
| *"Where can I take a forklift course?"* | When the guide has nothing verified, it says so plainly and refers to KIS and 2-1-1. It does not guess. |
| Ask in another language, e.g. Arabic: *"كيف أسجل طفلي في المدرسة؟"* | Questions can be in any language; the answer comes back in that language. |

## One question to ask KIS

> "Would something like this be useful for your staff, or for your clients — and what would you need to see before you would feel comfortable with it?"

## If something goes wrong

- **The answer is slow (5–15 seconds):** that is normal. The page shows its progress.
- **"Today's questions are used up":** the daily test limit has been reached. Use the Life Guide directly; Neil can raise the limit.
- **An answer looks wrong:** say so openly. Every answer shows its sources and check dates so errors can be traced. Press **Wrong** and add a sentence; that feedback is how it improves.
