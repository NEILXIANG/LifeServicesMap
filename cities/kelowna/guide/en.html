🏙️ About {CITY}

{CITY} sits on the east shore of Okanagan Lake in BC's southern Interior, on the traditional, unceded territory of the Syilx (Okanagan) people. West {CITY} and Westbank are across the lake, joined to the city by the bridge on Highway 97 — this guide covers both sides.

✈️ Arriving at the Airport

{CITY} International Airport (YLW) is at the north end of the city, next to UBC Okanagan. By bus, take route 23 from the terminal to UBCO Exchange, then RapidBus 97 to Downtown or across the bridge to West {CITY} — allow about 35–50 minutes to Downtown. On weekdays route 23 stops at the terminal from early morning until after midnight; on Saturdays less often, until about 9 pm. In the October–December 2026 timetable, no bus stops at the terminal on Sundays — the nearest stops are on Airport Way, several hundred metres away, or take a taxi. Licensed taxis wait at the front curb of the terminal; ride-hailing (Uber, Uride) picks up at the north end of the terminal. Timetables change: check {TRANSIT}’s trip planner before you fly.

🧭 Your First Stop: Local Settlement Services

If you have time for exactly one thing, make it KCR Community Resources – Immigrant Services (Bernard Avenue, downtown; 250-763-8008, Immigrant Services is extension 1; find it under Category → Gov/Immigration). Settlement services for permanent residents and refugees are free; if you are on a work or study permit, a refugee claimant or a new citizen, ask about BC’s own settlement program (BC NSP). Two other cheap first steps: a free library card at any Okanagan Regional Library branch, and the WorkBC centres for job search help.

🩺 Seeing a Doctor Without a Family Doctor

Most people who have just arrived start without a family doctor. Use an Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC) — there is one in Rutland, one in West {CITY} and one downtown — or a walk-in clinic (Category → Medical). For advice any time, call 8-1-1 (HealthLink BC, nurses and interpreters). For an emergency, call 9-1-1 or go to {CITY} General Hospital on Pandosy Street, open 24 hours.

🚌 Getting Around

Buses are run by {TRANSIT}. Routes meet at exchanges — Queensway downtown, Orchard Park, Rutland, UBC Okanagan and Okanagan College (Category → Transit). The city stretches a long way north–south along the lake, so check the trip time before you rely on a connection.

🏫 Schools

Public schools are run by {SD}. Your catchment school depends on your home address — look it up on the district's catchment page before you choose where to rent.

🌡️ Seasons

Summer is hot and dry, and wildfire smoke is common from July to September. Check the air quality before outdoor plans and sign up for the region's emergency alerts (Central Okanagan Emergency Management, through Alertable). Winter brings snow and ice on the hills; winter tires or chains are required on most BC highways from October 1 to April 30.

💰 Free Resources

Food support: the Central Okanagan Food Bank (Enterprise Way). Free legal help: Legal Aid BC at the Law Courts and the Okanagan Thompson Legal Clinic (Category → Legal Aid). Library: free card, computers and printing, children's programmes.
