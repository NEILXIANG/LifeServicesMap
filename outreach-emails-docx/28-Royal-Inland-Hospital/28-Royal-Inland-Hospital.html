Hi,

I maintain a free, six-language guide to Kamloops life-admin, started out of my own family's experience settling in here. One card specifically addresses being sick without a family doctor — when to call 8-1-1, when to go to North Shore UPCC, and when it's a 911 situation — and another covers the MSP waiting period. QR code attached (qr-sick.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=sick.

If it's ever useful for hospital or social work staff to have on hand for a newcomer patient, I wanted you to know it exists, and I'd welcome staying in touch.
