Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops, we were full of hope for this new chapter — but also, like most newcomers, a little lost and unsettled in a brand new place. Wanting to understand this city and settle in well myself, I started putting together what became the Kamloops Life Guide, and I hope to keep building on it so it helps more than just my own family.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected — covering 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards. The card for people who can't cover food or rent this month leads with your food bank, including that ID isn't used to screen immigration status, and the different days for families vs. singles/couples, with an earlier slot for seniors. I just added a note pointing people to Mount Paul Community Food Centre and The Salvation Army too, since the three of you run on different days — so if yours doesn't work for someone today, another might. QR code attached (qr-hardship.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=hardship.

I wanted to check all of that is still accurate, since hours and schedules go stale fastest. Attached (or happy to send) is a print-ready flyer with its own QR code, plus a short overview — either could be useful for your waiting area. I'd love to stay in touch as this grows.

Attached materials:

  • QR code image (qr-hardship.png) — scan to open the guide directly to the relevant card
  • 1-page flyer — a six-language, one-page summary of the guide with its own QR code, easy to print or post
  • 2-page overview — a plain-language summary of what the guide covers
