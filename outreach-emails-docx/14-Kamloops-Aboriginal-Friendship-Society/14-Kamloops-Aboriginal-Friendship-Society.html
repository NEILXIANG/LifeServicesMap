Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops we were hopeful but also a little lost, so I started building what's become the Kamloops Life Guide, a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected.

It includes your friendship centre as a resource for Indigenous residents — status, non-status, Métis and Inuit — noting the services you run and that it's best to call ahead. QR code attached (qr-main.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev. I want to check that description is accurate and respectful, and I'd very much welcome knowing if there's anything you'd want changed about how it's worded.

I'd also welcome any correction to how the guide's history section discusses Tk'emlúps te Secwépemc and the residential school history — accuracy and voice there matter more than almost anything else in the guide, and it isn't my place to be the final word on it. I'd genuinely value staying in touch, especially if there's ever a way to make sure this reflects your community's own voice more directly rather than just mine. I'm happy to send a written overview too — a plain-language summary of what the guide covers — if that's a more comfortable way to look it over than the live site.
