Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops, we were full of hope for this new chapter — but also, like most newcomers, a little lost and unsettled in a brand new place. Wanting to understand this city and settle in well myself, I started putting together what became the Kamloops Life Guide, and I've kept building on it since.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here (health cards, SIN, tenant rights, and so on) — no account, no ads, no personal data collected, works right in the browser, no download needed. Your branch is already named in it as a place newcomers should know about. QR code attached (qr-main.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev.

I have a one-page flyer with its own QR code, good for a front desk or bulletin board, plus a short overview (a two-page walkthrough of what the guide covers and how it works) — happy to send either, and happy to stay in touch if it's ever handy to have a link ready when someone at the desk asks "where do I even start."

Attached materials:

  • QR code image (qr-main.png) — scan to open the guide directly to the relevant card
  • 1-page flyer — a six-language, one-page summary of the guide with its own QR code, easy to print or post
  • 2-page overview — a plain-language summary of what the guide covers
