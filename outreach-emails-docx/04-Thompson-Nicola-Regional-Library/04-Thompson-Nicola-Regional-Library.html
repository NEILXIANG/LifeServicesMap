Hi Margo,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops, we were full of hope for this new chapter — but also, like most newcomers, a little lost and unsettled in a brand new place. Wanting to understand this city and settle in well myself, I started putting together what became the Kamloops Life Guide. It's helped me a great deal, and I hope to keep building on it over time, so it can be useful not just for newcomers, but also for people who have already made Kamloops home.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected — covering 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards, with an interactive map built in, right in the browser on any phone. One card ("I don't know anyone here") points readers to the library specifically: free programs, wifi, meeting rooms, and staff who know what's on. Want to see it? QR code attached (qr-connect.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=connect.

I'd like it to actually reflect what each branch offers rather than a generic line, so if you or anyone on your team ever has five minutes to tell me what I'm missing about the Kamloops or North Kamloops branches, I'd genuinely welcome it. Attached (or happy to send) are five short PDFs — a one-page flyer with its own QR code (Letter size, good for a community board), a two-page overview, a fuller introduction, a quick-start cheat sheet, and the full user guide. I'd love to stay in touch as this grows, and if there's ever a way it could help more of the people who come through your branches, I'm all ears.

Attached materials:

  • QR code image (qr-connect.png) — scan to open the guide directly to the relevant card
  • 1-page flyer — a six-language, one-page summary of the guide with its own QR code, easy to print or post
  • 2-page overview — a plain-language summary of what the guide covers
  • 5-page introduction — a fuller description of the guide and how it works
  • Quick-start cheat sheet — how to use the guide in under a minute
  • Full user guide — the complete walkthrough of every feature
