A free local guide — and an idea for international students
↓ 从这里开始选中，复制到 Outlook 正文 ↓

Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops, we were full of hope for this new chapter — but also, like most newcomers, a little lost and unsettled in a brand new place. I started putting together what became the Kamloops Life Guide, and I hope to keep building on it over time.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — health cards, SIN, tenant rights, that kind of thing — aimed mainly at newcomers generally, no account, no ads, no personal data collected. QR code attached (qr-main.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev. It occurred to me that international students have a specific version of this problem (the work-hour limit, the gap between school insurance and MSP coverage, credential recognition) that the guide doesn't yet address as its own card.

I'd be glad to build a dedicated "international student" card if TRU World has an interest in it being accurate and worth pointing students to — you'd know better than I would what actually trips students up, and I'd love for this to become an ongoing conversation rather than a one-off email. Attached (or happy to send) are five short PDFs — a flyer, two overview lengths, and a full user guide — if any would be useful to have on hand at your office.

Attached materials:

  • QR code image (qr-main.png) — scan to open the guide directly to the relevant card
  • 1-page flyer — a six-language, one-page summary of the guide with its own QR code, easy to print or post
  • 2-page overview — a plain-language summary of what the guide covers
  • 5-page introduction — a fuller description of the guide and how it works
  • Quick-start cheat sheet — how to use the guide in under a minute
  • Full user guide — the complete walkthrough of every feature
