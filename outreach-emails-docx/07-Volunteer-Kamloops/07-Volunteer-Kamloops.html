When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops we were hopeful but also a little lost, so I started building what's become the Kamloops Life Guide, and I've kept adding to it since.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected — covering 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards, with an interactive map built in. The card for newcomers who don't know anyone here leads with volunteering through your organization, both as a way to meet people and as where Canadian references and local work history often start. QR code attached (qr-connect.png), or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=connect.
