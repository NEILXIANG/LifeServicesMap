When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops we were hopeful but also a little lost, so I started building what's become the Kamloops Life Guide, and I've kept adding to it since, hoping it can help more than just my own family.

In short, it's a free, six-language reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected — covering 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards, with an interactive map built in. 2-1-1 is quoted throughout it as the answer to "I don't know what kind of problem this even is" — one of a handful of numbers shown as one-tap phone links wherever someone might need them, including a block that surfaces automatically if everything else looks closed.
