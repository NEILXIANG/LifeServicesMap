A multilingual local guide, built out of my own family's first year here
↓ 从这里开始选中，复制到 Outlook 正文 ↓

Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops, we were full of hope for this new chapter — but also, like most newcomers, a little lost and unsettled in a brand new place. Wanting to understand this city and settle in well myself, I started putting together what became the Kamloops Life Guide. It's helped me a great deal, and I hope to keep building on it over time, so it can be useful not just for newcomers, but also for people who have already made Kamloops home.

In short, the Kamloops Life Guide is a free reference for everyday life here that I put together myself — no account, no ads, no personal data collected. It currently covers 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards, all fully translated into six languages, with an interactive map built in. It works right in the browser on any phone — no download needed.

Kamloops Immigrant Services is already the single highest-rated resource in it, and I recently added specific mentions of your Conversation Circles, the SWIS program, and SPOKE directly on the relevant cards, since those are exactly the kind of thing someone reading about registering a child for school or filing a human rights complaint should know exists. Want to see it in action? I've attached a QR code (qr-connect.png) that opens straight to the "I don't know anyone here" card — or the link is https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=connect.

Attached (or happy to send) are five short PDFs that give a fuller picture: a one-page flyer with its own QR code, a two-page overview, a fuller five-page introduction, a quick-start cheat sheet, and the full user guide. I'd genuinely love to stay in touch as this grows — if there's ever a way it could help more of the people who come through your door, I'm all ears, and if anything about how I've described your programs is off, please tell me and I'll fix it the same day.

Attached materials:

  • 1-page flyer — a six-language, one-page summary of the guide with its own QR code, easy to print or post

  • 5-page introduction — a fuller description of the guide and how it works

  • Full user guide — the complete walkthrough of every feature
