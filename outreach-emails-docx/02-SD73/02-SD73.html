Hi,

When my family and I first arrived in Kamloops we were hopeful but also a little lost, so I started building what's become the Kamloops Life Guide — and I've kept adding to it since, hoping it can help more than just my own family.

In short, it's a free reference for everyday life here — no account, no ads, no personal data collected — covering 287 verified places and 14 practical task cards in six languages, with an interactive map built in, right in the browser, no download needed.

The card about registering a child for school now specifically mentions SWIS — that a newcomer family can ask the school about a free, in-school settlement worker. I've attached a QR code (qr-school.png) straight to that card, or https://kamloops-lifemap.neilxiang.workers.dev/?task=school.

I'd welcome staying in touch — both so you know this exists, and so you can tell me if the description doesn't match how SWIS actually works day to day. Happy to send a one-page flyer (a six-language summary of the guide, with its own QR code) or a short overview (what the guide covers and how it works) too, if either's useful to have on hand.
